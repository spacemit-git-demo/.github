## Who We Are

SpacemiT: Founded in November 2021, we are a RISC-V architecture AI CPU chip company. We independently develop full-stack core technologies to provide a range of chip products and computing systems, including terminal AI CPUs, cloud AI CPUs, and cloud AI inference. Our mission is to advance and perfect the RISC-V + AI + Triton/Tilelang intelligent computing ecosystem, driving innovation in applications such as AI computers and AI robots.

进迭时空成立于2021年11月，是一家RISC-V架构AI CPU芯片企业。自研全栈核心技术，提供终端AI CPU、云端AI CPU、云端AI推理等芯片产品和计算系统，推动和完善RISC-V+AI+Triton/Tilelang智算生态，助力AI计算机、AI机器人等创新应用的发展。

## Target Markets

- **Agent Computer** — On-device LLM reasoning, task planning, tool use and local intelligent agents.
- **Robot Computer** — On-board perception, decision-making, motion control and embodied intelligence.
